What four of them can also do

A mark that is also the reading

Ring, Tank, Dial and Bar each hold a value, so the favicon in your tab strip can be the live session window — you would know you were at 91% without opening the tab. Colour appears only at the threshold; the rest of the time the mark is plain ink. When the collector goes stale the mark loses its value rather than showing a wrong one, which is the same rule the dashboard already follows.

If you want one answer

  • 01 · Ring. Clearest of the twelve at 16px, perfectly square, about twenty lines of SVG, and the favicon can carry your real session window — the one genuinely useful thing a tab-strip icon could do for this tool. Nothing about it needs explaining.
  • 02 · Tank if you want it heavier and less common; 04 · Break if you would rather the mark say rate and stay a fixed drawing.
  • Whichever you pick, ship it as two files the way you do now: the mark on the 24 grid, and a favicon redrawn on a true 16 grid with thicker strokes so the edges land on whole pixels.

All twelve are drawn on a 24-unit grid with 2–2.6 unit strokes, inherit currentColor, and use no gradients, no texture and no more than two paths. The 16px renders on this page are the real thing at real size, not scaled screenshots.
